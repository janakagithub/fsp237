Which integration method best reproduces S1? rank-normalized composite of Spearman ρ, hi-expr recall, flux precision, and MCC (active vs expressed)
What do these metrics mean?
Spearman ρ
Spearman rank correlation between |flux| and aggregated S1 expression (log2 TPM+1) across GPR reactions. +1 = higher-expressed reactions carry more flux; 0 = no monotonic relation.
hi_recall
Highly-expressed recall: fraction of "hi" expression-bin reactions that carry active flux (|v| > 1e-6). How fully the model uses the genes it expresses most.
flux_prec
Flux precision: of reactions carrying active flux, the fraction that are expressed (bin hi or med). High = little flux through lowly/absent-expressed reactions.
MCC
Matthews correlation coefficient of active (|v| > 1e-6) vs expressed (bin hi/med) — a balanced binary-agreement score in [-1, 1], robust to class imbalance.
composite
Mean of the four rank-normalized components above. The overall method-vs-S1 concordance score used for ranking.
