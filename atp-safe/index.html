Methods. Stage 0 aggregates gene expression to reactions via GPR (max-of-OR / min-of-AND). Stage 1 overlays the aggregated expression on parsimonious FBA (pFBA) fluxes per condition. Stage 2 runs three techniques: E-Flux (continuous flux caps), GIMME (LP penalising low-expression flux under a biomass floor), and iMAT (MILP maximising high↔active + low↔inactive). Stage 4 triages orphan (no-GPR) reactions that carry flux for downstream BLAST-based gene assignment. MADE (Stage 3) is deferred pending additional biological groups.
